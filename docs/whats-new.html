What's new in Replay Desk

Every update is free for license holders. Replay Desk tells you when a new version is ready; click Update now or turn on Install updates automatically in Settings. Installers for each version are also on GitHub.

Version 1.3.2 November 6, 2026

  • Smoother replays in demanding games: capture now gets priority on your graphics card, so it keeps grabbing frames even when a game uses 100% of the GPU.

Version 1.3.1 November 6, 2026

  • Fixed updates sometimes downloading but not installing. Updates now install and reopen the app by themselves.

Version 1.3.0 November 6, 2026

  • Replay Desk now has a 14-day free trial, then a one-time $14.99 lifetime license.
  • Buy and unlock right inside the app: click Unlock, then paste the license key from your email.
  • One key works on up to 3 of your PCs. Move it any time from Settings.

Version 1.2.4 November 6, 2026

  • New Settings tab in the side overlay: change replay length, quality, frame rate, game audio, mic, sound effects and buffer-at-startup without leaving your game.

Version 1.2.3 November 6, 2026

  • Updates finish cleanly: the old version closes and the new one opens, with no error box.
  • Desktop and Start menu icons refresh to the new logo after updating.

Version 1.2.2 November 6, 2026

  • New Replay Desk logo and app icon across the app, tray, installer and overlay.
  • Refreshed colours to match the new logo.

Version 1.2.1 November 6, 2026

  • New side panel overlay that slides in from the right edge of the screen, with Save replay, Record, the buffer switch, a live recording timer and your last saved clip. Works over fullscreen and borderless games without taking focus.
  • Prefer the small floating panel? Choose it under Settings → Overlay style.

Version 1.2.0 November 5, 2026

  • New: Send to phone. Scan a QR code and download any video to your phone over your home Wi-Fi. No cables, accounts or uploads.
  • Big videos are turned into a phone-friendly 1080p copy automatically.
  • Each link only works for the video you picked and expires after an hour.

Version 1.1.1 November 4, 2026

  • New Move to main screen button brings the floating overlay back if it ends up off-screen.
  • The overlay now positions correctly on high-DPI and multi-monitor setups.
  • Update checks every hour.

Version 1.1.0 November 4, 2026

  • New in-game overlay with Save replay, Record and buffer controls. It stays on top of your game and doesn't steal focus.
  • Starting a recording now starts capture automatically if the buffer was off.

Version 1.0.0 October 31, 2026

  • First release: replay buffer with a save hotkey, full-session recording, GPU capture with game audio and mic on separate tracks, a clip editor with multiple segments, compilations with cut, fade and crossfade transitions, and automatic update checks.
