What's new in Replay Desk

Every update is free for license holders. Replay Desk tells you when a new version is ready; click Update now or turn on Install updates automatically in Settings. Installers for each version are also on GitHub.

Feature updates change the middle number (1.5 → 1.6). Fix updates only change the last number (1.6.0 → 1.6.1). Each change is marked New, Improved or Fixed. A small tag like 1.6.1 shows which update a change came in.

Version 1.6 Latest1.6.0 – 1.6.1 · November 11, 2026

New

  • Vertical videos for Shorts, TikTok and Reels can zoom out. Keep the whole screen in frame or just the part you want, with a live phone-shaped preview.
  • Fill the space above and below a vertical video with a blurred copy of your gameplay, or plain black.
  • Library: switch between tile view and a new list view. Replay Desk remembers your choice.

Fixed

  • The blurred background on vertical videos could show green and black halves instead of a blur. If you made vertical videos with 1.6.0, make them again. 1.6.1

Version 1.5 1.5.0 – 1.5.1 · November 11, 2026

New

  • Library: sort by newest, oldest, longest, largest or name, and see how many videos you have.

Improved

  • A fresh new look across the whole app, with page headers, rounded filter pills and cleaner cards on every screen.
  • Library: hover a video to see Edit, Rename, Share and Send to phone. Show in folder and Delete are in the ••• menu.
  • Capture and Compile show the buffer status and your compilation's clip count and length at a glance.

Fixed

  • Screens weren't centered on wide and ultrawide monitors. 1.5.1

Version 1.4 1.4.0 – 1.4.1 · November 11, 2026

New

  • Share button on every video in the Library, and after exporting a clip or compilation.
  • Make a copy that fits where you're posting it: YouTube (full quality), Shorts · TikTok · Reels (vertical 1080×1920), Discord (under the 20 MB free limit), Email (under 25 MB) and X (1080p, 60 fps).
  • Share… opens the Windows share menu with the video attached, so you can send it to Mail, Outlook, Nearby Share, WhatsApp and other apps.
  • Upload to YouTube opens YouTube's upload page in your browser, shows the video in its folder ready to drag in, and copies a suggested title.

Improved

  • The Share window shows each site's logo: YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram, Discord, X and email. 1.4.1

Fixed

  • Clicking the icon on the Send to phone button could open the editor instead. 1.4.1

Version 1.3 1.3.0 – 1.3.2 · November 6, 2026

New

  • A 14-day free trial, then a one-time $14.99 lifetime license.
  • Buy and unlock right inside the app: click Unlock, then paste the license key from your email.
  • One key works on up to 3 of your PCs. Move it any time from Settings.

Improved

  • Smoother replays in demanding games: capture now gets priority on your graphics card, so it keeps grabbing frames even when a game uses 100% of the GPU. 1.3.2

Fixed

  • Updates sometimes downloaded but didn't install. Updates now install and reopen the app by themselves. 1.3.1

Version 1.2 1.2.0 – 1.2.4 · November 5–6, 2026

New

  • Send to phone: scan a QR code and download any video to your phone over your home Wi-Fi. No cables, accounts or uploads.
  • Big videos are turned into a phone-friendly 1080p copy automatically. Each link only works for the video you picked and expires after an hour.
  • Side panel overlay that slides in from the right edge of the screen, with Save replay, Record, the buffer switch, a live recording timer and your last saved clip. Prefer the small floating panel? Choose it under Settings → Overlay style. 1.2.1
  • Settings tab in the side overlay: change replay length, quality, frame rate, game audio, mic, sound effects and buffer-at-startup without leaving your game. 1.2.4

Improved

  • New Replay Desk logo, app icon and colours across the app, tray, installer and overlay. 1.2.2

Fixed

  • Updating showed an error box and left the old version open. The old version now closes and the new one opens by itself. 1.2.3
  • Desktop and Start menu icons didn't refresh to the new logo after updating. 1.2.3

Version 1.1 1.1.0 – 1.1.1 · November 4, 2026

New

  • In-game overlay with Save replay, Record and buffer controls. It stays on top of your game and doesn't steal focus.
  • Move to main screen button brings the floating overlay back if it ends up off-screen. 1.1.1

Improved

  • Starting a recording now starts capture automatically if the buffer was off.
  • Checks for updates every hour. 1.1.1

Fixed

  • The overlay could appear in the wrong place on high-DPI and multi-monitor setups. 1.1.1

Version 1.0 1.0.0 · October 31, 2026

New

  • First release: replay buffer with a save hotkey, full-session recording, GPU capture with game audio and mic on separate tracks, a clip editor with multiple segments, compilations with cut, fade and crossfade transitions, and automatic update checks.
