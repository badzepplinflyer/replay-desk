Release notes and previous versions

System requirements

Windows
Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
Graphics card
Recommended: an NVIDIA card (NVENC encoder) or AMD card (AMF encoder), so your graphics card's built-in video encoder does the recording. PCs without either can record with the x264 (CPU) encoder.
Encoder setting
Replay Desk starts on the NVIDIA encoder. On an AMD card choose AMD AMF (GPU), or choose x264 (CPU), under Settings → Video → Encoder.
Disk space
Room for the app plus your videos. The replay buffer and your saved videos take more space at higher frame rates, quality settings and replay lengths.
Internet
Only needed to download, check for updates and activate a license. Recording works offline.

Install

  1. Run ReplayDesk-Setup.exe. It installs for your Windows user account only, so it doesn't ask for administrator permission.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new apps from small developers; you'll only see it once.
  3. Choose whether you want a desktop shortcut and whether Replay Desk should start in the tray when Windows starts. You can change your mind later.
  4. Click Install. Replay Desk opens when it's done.

Your first replay

  1. The replay buffer starts automatically. On the Capture screen, pick your display, frame rate (30, 60 or 120 fps), quality and replay length (30 seconds to 20 minutes).
  2. Set the hotkeys you want for saving a replay, starting and stopping a recording, and showing the overlay. Every hotkey can be changed in Settings.
  3. Play your game. When something worth keeping happens, press your save replay hotkey and the last stretch of gameplay is saved as an MP4.
  4. Your videos are saved in Videos\Replay Desk, sorted into Replays, Recordings, Clips and Compilations. You can choose a different folder in Settings.

Trial and license

The trial includes every feature for 14 days. To keep recording and exporting after that, buy a lifetime license for $14.99. Your license key arrives by email; in Replay Desk click Unlock at the top of the window, paste the key and click Activate. One license works on up to 3 of your PCs, and all future updates are included. More details are in the pricing section.

Updates

Replay Desk checks for new versions on its own and shows what's new when one is ready. Update now installs it and reopens the app; your settings, hotkeys and videos are kept. You can also turn on automatic updates in Settings. Every change is listed on the What's new page.

Uninstall

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Replay Desk and choose Uninstall. Your saved videos in Videos\Replay Desk are left where they are.
