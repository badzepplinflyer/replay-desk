How the free trial works

Every feature is unlocked for 14 days from the first time you open Replay Desk. The top of the app shows how many days are left. When the trial ends, recording, saving replays, exporting and sending to your phone pause until you add a license key. Your saved videos stay where they are, and you can still open and watch them.

Buying a license

A lifetime license is $14.99, paid once. Click Unlock at the top of Replay Desk, or buy from the website. Checkout is handled by Polar, our payment provider. Your license key is emailed to you right away and starts with RD-. It never expires and includes all future updates.

Activating your key

  1. In Replay Desk, click Unlock at the top of the window (or next to License in Settings).
  2. Paste your key exactly as it appears in the email and click Activate.
  3. Activation needs an internet connection. After that, Replay Desk only re-checks the key every few days, and being offline never locks you out.

If activation fails

  • "That key wasn't found": copy the key again from your email, including the RD- at the start, with no spaces before or after.
  • "Already active on the maximum number of PCs": your key is in use on 3 PCs. Remove it from one of them (see below), then try again.
  • "Couldn't reach the license server": check your internet connection, then try again.
  • "That key has been disabled": this happens after a refund. If you think it's a mistake, email support.

Using it on more than one PC

One license works on up to 3 of your PCs at the same time. Activate the same key on each one.

Moving to a new PC

On the old PC, open Settings and click Remove from this PC next to License, then activate the key on the new PC. If the old PC is gone or won't start, sign in to the customer portal with your purchase email; you can remove PCs from your key there.

Lost your key?

Search your email for "Polar" or "Replay Desk". You can also sign in to the customer portal with the email you bought with: it shows your key and receipt. Still can't find it? Email support from that address.

Do I have to pay for updates?

No. Your license includes every future version. Replay Desk tells you when an update is ready, and you can see what changed on the What's new page.

Contact

For anything about your purchase or key, email support@getreplaydesk.com (support@getreplaydesk.com) from the address you bought with. For problems with recording, see Troubleshooting.
