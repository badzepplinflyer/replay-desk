Troubleshooting

Capture won't start or keeps stopping

If the Capture screen shows an error, or the buffer keeps switching off:

  1. Check the encoder matches your graphics card. Replay Desk starts on the NVIDIA encoder. On an AMD card choose AMD AMF (GPU); on a PC without an NVIDIA or AMD card choose x264 (CPU). It's under Settings → Video → Encoder.
  2. Check the display. If you have more than one monitor, pick the one your game is on under Settings → Video → Display.
  3. Changing resolution is fine. When a game switches resolution or display mode, capture restarts by itself within a few seconds.
  4. If the error mentions FFmpeg not being found, reinstall Replay Desk from the download page. Your settings and videos are kept.

Replays look choppy, but the game felt smooth

This usually means your graphics card was working at 100%, which leaves Windows little room to capture frames. The recording then gets fewer new frames than your game showed.

  1. Update Replay Desk. Version 1.3.2 and later give the capture higher priority on your graphics card.
  2. Cap your game's frame rate a little below what it normally reaches, so the graphics card isn't maxed out.
  3. Record at 60 fps instead of 120 (Settings → Video → Frame rate). That halves the capture work.
  4. Turn on Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling in Windows Settings → System → Display → Graphics → Change default graphics settings, then restart your PC.

No game sound in my videos

  1. Make sure Record game / PC audio is ticked under Settings → Audio.
  2. Set Output device to the speakers or headset you actually hear the game through. Windows default follows whatever Windows is set to.
  3. Play something and watch the Game audio meter on the Capture screen. If it moves, sound is being captured.

My microphone isn't recorded, or it's too loud or quiet

  1. Tick Record microphone and choose your mic under Settings → Audio → Microphone.
  2. Check that Windows lets desktop apps use your mic: Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → Let desktop apps access your microphone.
  3. Adjust Mic level in Settings for future recordings. Your mic is also saved on its own track, so you can turn it up, down or off for any clip in the editor.

Sound and video are out of sync

Move the Audio sync offset slider under Settings → Audio (from −300 to +300 ms) until sound lines up with the picture, then save a new replay to check.

A hotkey doesn't work

Under Settings, each hotkey shows ✓ Active when it's working. If it says the key is in use by another app (for example the NVIDIA overlay or Discord), pick a different combination. Any combination you like works.

Turn on Beep when saving in Settings to hear a confirmation each time a hotkey saves a replay.

The overlay doesn't appear over my game

  • Most games work, in fullscreen, borderless and windowed modes. A few older games use an exclusive fullscreen mode where Windows doesn't allow anything on top. Switch the game to borderless to use the overlay; your hotkeys still save replays and start recordings either way.
  • If you use the floating panel and it's gone off-screen or onto another monitor, click Move to main screen in Settings.

My phone can't open the Send to phone link

  1. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as your PC (not mobile data).
  2. The first time, Windows asks whether Replay Desk can use the network: click Allow.
  3. If it still can't connect, set your Wi-Fi to a Private network in Windows Settings → Network & internet.
  4. Each link works for one hour. For large videos, wait a moment while the phone-friendly copy is made.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Windows shows this for new apps from small developers. Click More info, then Run anyway. You'll only see it once per installer.

An update downloaded but didn't install

Download the latest installer from the download page and run it. It installs over your current version and keeps your settings, hotkeys, license and videos.

Where are my videos?

In Videos\Replay Desk, sorted into Replays, Recordings, Clips and Compilations. The Library tab in the app shows them all. To save somewhere else, change Save to folder in Settings.

Still stuck?

Email support@getreplaydesk.com (support@getreplaydesk.com) with what happened, your Replay Desk version (shown in Settings) and, if you can, the log file. To find it, press Win+R, paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\ReplayDesk and attach replaydesk.log. It lists what the app did, not your videos.
