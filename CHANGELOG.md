# Changelog

## 1.2.3
- Updates now finish cleanly: the old version closes on its own, the new one opens — no error box.
- Desktop and Start menu icons refresh to the new logo after updating.

## 1.2.2
- New Replay Desk logo and app icon, now on the desktop shortcut, Start menu, taskbar, tray and installer.
- Refreshed colours to match the new logo throughout the app, the overlay and the Send to phone page.

## 1.2.1
- New side panel overlay: press your overlay hotkey and a control panel slides in from the right edge of the screen, with Save replay, Record, the buffer switch, a live recording timer and your last saved clip. Press it again and it slides away. Works over fullscreen and borderless games and never takes focus from your game.
- Prefer the small floating panel? Choose it under Settings → Overlay style.
- The floating panel's hotkey hint no longer gets cut off.

## 1.2.0
- New: Send to phone. Click the 📱 button on any video in the Library (or after exporting a clip or compilation), scan the QR code with your phone's camera, and download the video over your home Wi-Fi. No cables, accounts or cloud uploads.
- Big videos (1440p, 120 fps, multiple audio tracks) are turned into a phone-friendly 1080p copy automatically. Untick the option to send the original.
- Each link only works for the video you picked and expires after an hour. Nothing else on your PC is reachable.
- The first time, Windows may ask to let Replay Desk use the network: click Allow.

## 1.1.1
- Overlay: new "Move to main screen" button in Settings brings the panel back to the top-right of your main monitor if it ends up off-screen or on the wrong display.
- Overlay now positions correctly on high-DPI and multi-monitor setups.
- Replay Desk now checks for updates every hour (was every 6 hours).

## 1.1.0
- New floating overlay: press Ctrl+Alt+O in-game to pop up controls for Save replay, Record/Stop and Start/Stop buffer. It stays on top of your game, doesn't steal focus when you click it, and you can drag it anywhere (it remembers the spot).
- Also available from the Overlay button at the top of the app and the tray menu; change the hotkey or show it at startup in Settings.
- Record now starts the capture automatically if the buffer was off.

## 1.0.0
First release of Replay Desk.
- Replay buffer: save the last few minutes of gameplay with a hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+F10)
- Full-session recording (Ctrl+Alt+F9)
- GPU (NVENC) capture with game audio and mic on separate tracks
- Clip editor: trim, cut multiple segments, adjust game and mic volume
- Compile clips into one video with cut, fade or crossfade transitions
- Automatic update checks
